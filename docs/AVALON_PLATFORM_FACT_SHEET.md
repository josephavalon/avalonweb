# Avalon Vitality — Platform Fact Sheet

*Internal high-level brief · July 2026*

## What it is

Avalon Vitality is a mobile clinical wellness operating platform for customer booking, nurse execution, admin operations, memberships, inventory, CRM, payments, and events—built around Bay Area mobile IV and recovery services.

## Platform footprint

- **Customer experience:** Premium web storefront, service catalog, service-area validation, multi-step booking, intake, Stripe checkout, subscriptions, account management, billing, documents, and messaging.
- **Nurse platform:** Shift queue, appointment management, client records, visit timers, route/ETA visibility, communications, protocols, inventory and kit visibility, mileage, and field closeout workflows.
- **Admin command center:** Bookings, dispatch, routing, field operations, CRM, finance, inventory, kit management, credential tracking, training, reports, communications, and event operations.
- **Events and B2B:** Corporate wellness, hotels, private groups, festivals, event presales, QR redemption, timed service windows, group intake, and sponsor/VIP blocks.
- **Technical foundation:** React/Vite, Supabase, Vercel serverless APIs, Stripe, Acuity Scheduling, Attio CRM, Resend, structured analytics, privacy/security checks, and release QA tooling.

## Example workflows

1. **Individual booking:** Customer selects therapy, location, time, intake details, and payment; the platform coordinates scheduling and CRM handoff.
2. **Nurse visit:** Nurse claims a shift, reviews the patient and protocol, navigates to the appointment, runs the treatment timer, communicates with operations, and closes the visit.
3. **Festival recovery:** Presale page supports timed reservations, guest credentials, QR redemption, and capacity-controlled recovery windows.
4. **Private group activation:** Avalon can plan on-site recovery for offices, hotels, teams, launches, and VIP groups.
5. **Recurring customer:** Memberships support recurring recovery, credits, appointment planning, billing, and customer messaging.
6. **Operations control:** Admin can coordinate staff, supplies, appointments, credentials, financial workflows, and event demand.

## Current status

The product capability is broad and architecturally substantial. Final production readiness still depends on environment configuration, database migrations, credential rotation, clinical-data controls, vendor BAAs, live payment/scheduling verification, and staging failure drills.

**Platform snapshot:** approximately 79 routes, 100+ reusable components, 17 API surfaces, and a full consumer-to-operations workflow.
